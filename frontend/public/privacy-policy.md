# Lex Pickup Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Lex Pickup Pro helps members of our recreational soccer club manage player profiles, games, and attendance. We use Facebook Login to let members sign in and connect their club accounts.

## Information received from Facebook

When you sign in with Facebook, the app requests:

- **Email address:** to identify your club account and support account creation or linking.
- **Facebook user ID specific to this app:** to recognize your Facebook account on future sign-ins and keep it linked to the correct club account.
- **Name:** to display during account setup, or to name a new player profile when new-player registration is enabled.

The app requests Facebook's `email` and `public_profile` permissions for these purposes.

Facebook Login does not give this app your Facebook password. Our integration does not request your posts, photos, friends list, or Messenger messages, and does not post to Facebook on your behalf.

## Use and storage

We use this Facebook information for sign-in and club account setup and linking. The email address and Facebook identity link are stored with your club account. Your name is also stored during account setup and may be used for a new player profile. Facebook access tokens are used temporarily to retrieve the sign-in information and are not saved in the club database.

The app separately stores information supplied by members and club organizers, such as player profiles, team assignments, attendance, and match records. These club records are separate from the information requested through Facebook Login.

The app uses cookies to maintain sign-in and account-setup sessions.

## Privacy questions and deletion requests

Contact your club administrator through your usual club communication channel to ask about your information or request deletion of your account and Facebook-derived data. Provide the email address associated with your club account so the administrator can identify it and verify your request. Do not send your Facebook password or access tokens.

Removing Lex Pickup Pro from your Facebook app permissions does not by itself delete information already stored in the club app. Deactivating a player profile also preserves historical club records; ask explicitly for data deletion if that is your intention.
