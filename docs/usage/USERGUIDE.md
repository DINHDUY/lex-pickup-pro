# Lex Pickup Pro User Guide

This guide was captured in iPhone 17-sized mobile layout (430×932) using the live signed-in app at https://www.lex-pickup-pro.us.

## 1. Overview

The Overview screen is your home base. It gives you a quick snapshot of the next game, your RSVP status, and the club’s current activity.

![Overview](assets/overview.png)

Use it to:

- Check the next match on the pitch
- Review club updates and reminders
- Confirm whether you are going, maybe, or not going
- Jump straight to the schedule or recent match details

## 2. Schedule and match RSVP

The Schedule view shows upcoming fixtures, lets you filter by game type, and makes it easy to update your availability.

![Schedule](assets/schedule.png)

Use it to:

- Browse future games and derby matches
- Search for a match by title or date
- Change your availability before kickoff
- Open a full match details view from any listed game

## 3. Match center

The Match Center is where you see the game details, live team information, and the club’s attendance summary.

![Match center](assets/match-center.png)

Use it to:

- Review match location, time, and field information
- Share a game summary to Messenger
- Update your response for the game
- View the team sheet and overall attendance snapshot

## 4. Players and squad

The Players screen helps you find teammates, understand role coverage, and explore the roster.

![Players](assets/players.png)

Use it to:

- Search for players by name or nickname
- Filter the squad by team or assignment
- View player cards with availability and basic stats
- Open a teammate’s profile for more detail

## 5. Player profile

Each Player Profile shows the person behind the number, from availability to recent form.

![Player profile](assets/player-profile.png)

Use it to:

- Review a player’s preferred positions and details
- Check their attendance and recent performance data
- Understand their availability and status for upcoming matches
- Open deeper club records when needed

## 6. Teams

The Teams screen gives you a clear view of how the club is structured across the squad.

![Teams](assets/teams.png)

Use it to:

- See the club’s current teams and formations
- Compare different team groupings
- Quickly find which side a player belongs to

## 7. Availability

The Availability tool helps the captain and club members coordinate who is in, maybe, or out.

![Availability](assets/availability.png)

Use it to:

- Track general attendance across upcoming fixtures
- Check who has confirmed their spot
- Keep the group informed before match day

## 8. Analytics and statistics

The Analytics screen gives the club a simple readout of performance trends and key statistics.

![Analytics](assets/analytics.png)

Use it to:

- Review match and player statistics
- Understand progress over time
- Monitor club performance and team trends

## 9. Match history

The Match History screen summarises past fixtures and results so the group can look back at recent play.

![History](assets/history.png)

Use it to:

- Review previous games and outcomes
- Look back at recent club activity
- Revisit past match details when planning upcoming games

## 10. Club management

The Club screen is the home for club administration and community tools.

![Club](assets/club.png)

Use it to:

- Share your club invitation link
- Review club information and landing details
- Access records and season exports
- Keep the group connected through Messenger and the app experience

## Quick start

1. Open the app home page.
2. Check the next game on the Overview screen.
3. Use Schedule to confirm your availability.
4. Open Match Center for details and team sheet information.
5. Use Players to find teammates and view their profiles.
6. Visit Club to manage club information and seasonal records.

## Notes

- These screenshots reflect the current live app state and mobile layout.
- The app is optimized for quick mobile use before kickoff, during match planning, and for club communication.
